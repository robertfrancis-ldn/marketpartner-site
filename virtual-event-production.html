Three ways to produce, one crew behind all of them

Whether your event is virtual-only, streamed from the room, or broadcast from a studio, it runs on the same production standard and the same team from start to finish.

Virtual studio

Multi-location virtual events

Our virtual green room takes up to 20 remote speakers and producers, joining from anywhere in the world.

  • Global events run virtually from our London studio
  • Complete support, run of show to live stream
  • Technical tests and full rehearsals for speakers
  • 4K cameras, vision & audio mixing, auto-cue
Onsite streaming

Production of in-person events

Full AV support or a lighter streaming set-up, with our crew onsite for the whole event.

  • Camera operators, streaming engineer, sound engineer
  • Virtual producers directing the stream in real time
  • 4K cameras, lighting, auto-cue, monitors & screens
  • Onsite or remote, sized to the room
Studio broadcast

Production from a physical studio

Record and stream from a world-class studio for total peace of mind and the highest level of production quality.

  • Central London location, plus partner studios globally
  • Green room facilities and a full production crew
  • Green screens, auto-cue, comfort monitors
  • TV-quality production and broadcast
— Virtual audience

The most powerful platform for broadcasting to a global audience

Every stream runs on a viewing platform built to your brand, not a generic player — with the interactivity to keep a remote audience engaged for the full session.

  • Highly flexible viewing platform, designed to your requirements
  • Totally on-brand with your company colours and logo
  • Breakout room functionality for smaller sessions
  • Live chat, networking, questions and messaging
  • Polling, voting, word clouds and live quizzes built in
  • Feedback forms, resources and gamification
  • On-demand viewing once the live session ends
  • Real-time analytics on attendance and engagement

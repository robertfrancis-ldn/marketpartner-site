● Access

Quick and easy access via a QR code or shortlink URL

Access the app quickly and easily, save an icon and create a profile. Anonymous participation options also available.

92% of B2B event attendees use their company device at events, which often restrict downloads of unauthorized native apps — so a browser-based app people can reach in seconds matters more than it sounds.
● Live polling

Live polling, surveys and word clouds to make sessions highly interactive

  • Attendees actively participate and provide real-time feedback, share opinions and collaborate
  • Both remote and onsite attendees interact in real time
  • Highly effective in panel sessions for full audience engagement
  • Easy to operate from an AV perspective, with a full control panel

● Q&A & feedback

Take questions & comments from attendees for moderation

  • Moderators access attendee questions in real time, with the ability to organize and moderate
  • A highly effective way to field audience questions without microphones and delays
  • All questions logged for post-event follow-up
  • One or more moderators can access simultaneously

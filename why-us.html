● Built with AI, controlled by you

Harness the power of AI. Keep control of what matters.

Our sites and apps are built as editable templates, with placeholders we define for the content that changes. A technical site owner can shape the template itself directly with AI — layout, structure, new functionality — while the wider team updates agendas, speakers and registration content through a simple CMS, with no access to the underlying build and nothing they can accidentally break. You get the speed of AI-assisted development without your customer data, or your site, ever being at risk.
